おにぎり

8月 1, 2026

材料

  • 炊いたごはん2カップ
  • のり1枚
  • 焼き鮭（塩鮭）1切れ（粗くほぐしておく）
  • 塩

調理工程

  1. Wet your hands with water and sprinkle them with salt.
  2. Take a handful of warm rice and make a small well in the center.
  3. Put the flaked salmon into the well and cover it with rice.
  4. Gently press the rice into a triangle shape.
  5. Wrap the rice ball with a strip of nori and serve.
